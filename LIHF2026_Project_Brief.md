## 🎨 Design System

### Color Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Gold / Yellow | `#F5C400` |
| Background | Deep Black | `#0A0A0A` |
| Surface | Dark Charcoal | `#111111` |
| Card | Dark Grey | `#1A1A1A` |
| Text Primary | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Text Secondary | Light Grey | `#AAAAAA` |
| Accent Border | Gold | `#F5C400` |

### Typography
- **Display / Hero**: `Bebas Neue` or `Anton` — bold, impactful
- **Headings**: `Space Grotesk` Bold
- **Body**: `Space Grotesk` Regular / `Inter`
- **Accent Labels**: `Oswald` Caps

### Style Direction
- Modern HipHop Professional — Dark Mode
- 3D floating elements, parallax depth
- Glassmorphism cards with gold borders
- Grunge texture overlays (subtle)
- Animated counters and scroll reveals
- Clean, minimal layout — not cluttered
