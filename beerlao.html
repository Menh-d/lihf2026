✦ TWO OPEN QUALIFIERS • ONE PATHWAY TO THE LIHF FINAL STAGE ✦

COMPETITION INFORMATION

Two premier categories showcasing the raw energy of urban talent. Open to all aspiring artists across Laos, powered and supported by Beerlao Green.

CATEGORY 01 • MC BATTLE

RAP BATTLE 1 VS 1

OPEN TO EVERYONE • 1 VS 1 ELIMINATION

Direct lyrical face-off on the mic. Emcees trade rhyme schemes, punchlines, and rhythm delivery under heavy crowd energy.

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
LYRICS (ເນື້ອຮ້ອງ) FLOW (ຈັງຫວະ) FREESTYLE (ໄຫວພິກ) DELIVERY (ການຖ່າຍທອດ) STAGE PRESENCE + IMPACT
✦ Qualification: Top 2 Rap Competitors per venue advance to LIHF Final Stage.
CATEGORY 02 • CLUB PERFORMANCE

DJ + MC CROWD CONTROL

10-MIN CLUB PERFORMANCE • THEME: CROWD CONTROL

A 10-minute dynamic tandem showcase where a DJ and MC unite to ignite the dancefloor, test club rockin' skills, and drive peak venue excitement.

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
CREATIVITY (ຄວາມຄິດສ້າງສັນ) MUSICALITY (ຄວາມເປັນດົນຕີ) TECHNICALITY (ເຕັກນິກ) PERFORMANCE (ການສະແດງ) CROWD CONTROL (ການຄຸມເວທີ)
✦ Qualification: Top 2 DJ/MC acts per venue advance to LIHF Final Stage.

✦ ATTRACT · RESERVE · ORDER · RETURN ✦

AUDIENCE EXPECTATION & SALES STRATEGY

Planning target: 200–300 people per venue — totaling 800–1,200 visits across four weekly activations. Engineered to turn event buzz into direct Beerlao Green consumption.

STEP 01

ATTRACT

Event demand generated via TikTok cyphers, venue social channels, and artist announcements.

STEP 02

RESERVE

Exclusive "Green Tables" advance reservations with premium viewing direct to the DJ battle arena.

STEP 03

ORDER & ENGAGE

Group beverage bundles, event-only promotions, rapid ordering points, and MC callout specials.

STEP 04

RETURN

Next-stop incentives: stamp cards and wristbands granting VIP perks at the next venue and LIHF.

✦ SALES MECHANICS

  • • Green Table packages: Curated tables bundled with ice-cold Beerlao Green.
  • • Group beverage bundles: Special tower and bucket offerings for crews.
  • • Premium viewing tables: Guaranteed front-row sightlines to the battle.
  • • Visible ordering points: Sub-zero draft bars and fast-track service.
  • • MC/artist callouts: Real-time cheering and celebratory toasts from the stage.

✦ CORE MEASURED KPIs

  • • Attendance & Footfall: Total verified attendees per venue (Target: 200–300).
  • • Tables Reserved: 100% capacity utilization of Green Tables.
  • • Beerlao Green Units Sold: Cans, bottles, and draft liters consumed.
  • • Sales Per Attendee: Average beverage spend per customer.
  • • Repeat Attendance: Attendees traveling across multiple weekly stops.
