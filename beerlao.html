THE TRIFECTA: DANCE, MUSIC & BEERLAO

A powerhouse collaboration bringing together the grassroots urban culture pioneer of Laos, the nation's premier record production label, and the most iconic beverage empire of the Mekong.

Cultural Pioneer & Festival Founder

Fanglao Dance Studio

Founded by Ounla Phaoudom, Fanglao is the heartbeat of contemporary and street dance in Laos. Host of international dance battles, creative residency programs, and official organizer of LIHF 2026.

HipHop Credibility Olympic Breaking Network Youth Loyalty
Music Titan & Sound Engineering

Indee Records & Events

Led by Anulak Prathammavong ("Aai Ped"), Indee Records has shaped modern Lao pop and rock for over two decades (producing Cells, Sam Intharaphithak, Taiy Akard). Masterminds of major national music festivals and audio experiences.

Lao Music Industry Lead Celebrity & DJ Roster Concert Sound Scale
Title Sponsor & National Icon

Beerlao Green (LBC)

Launched for Lao Brewery Company's 50th Anniversary, Beerlao Green is brewed with climate-resilient single-origin Hom Xebangfai rice. Crisp, refreshing (4.6% ABV), sustainable, and designed directly for dynamic youth lifestyle and celebrations.

Hom Xebangfai Rice 4.6% Ultra-Crisp National Pride
✦ CURATED FESTIVAL EXPERIENCE ✦

DJ CHAMPIONSHIPS & BEERLAO ACTIVATIONS

Four distinct high-octane zones engineered to maximize attendee dwell time, social media sharing, draft beer consumption, and brand affinity.

LIHF Culture Pillar #05

LIHF ASEAN DJ & Turntablism Championship

The premier scratch showdown of Laos. 16 top turntablists from Laos, Thailand, Vietnam, and Singapore compete on dual vinyl decks in high-stakes 2-minute sudden-death scratch battles.

  • Official Beerlao DJ Trophy + 25,000,000 LAK Prize Pool
  • Judged by Indee Records producers & ASEAN DMC World Champions
  • Free daytime Turntable Scratch Clinic for aspiring Lao youth
Beerlao Exclusive Activation

Beerlao Green Draft Master Challenge

An interactive audience challenge where festival attendees compete in rapid, foam-perfect draft pouring under the guidance of master brewmasters, winning exclusive co-branded merchandise.

  • 4 Ice-Cold Rapid Pour Draft Stations serving sub-zero Beerlao Green
  • Sensory tasting flight highlighting single-origin Hom Xebangfai rice
  • Collector's Edition Beerlao Green × LIHF 2026 frosted acrylic steins
Nightlife Social Hub

Urban Beer Garden & Emerald Lounge

A 2,000+ sqm social sanctuary seamlessly blending wooden high-top tables, illuminated artificial turf lawns, lush greenery, and ambient bistro string lights with direct DJ stage sightlines.

  • Capacity for 1,200+ seated festival guests with tableside mobile ordering
  • Curated Lao street food pairings designed to complement Beerlao Green
  • VIP Cabana Section reserved for Beerlao executives and artist guests
Viral Content Engine

Green Mascot Meet & 360 UGC Video Studio

The official life-size Boombox Green Mascot takes center stage for dance-offs and meet-and-greets, flanked by a 360-degree rotating slow-motion camera studio wrapped in emerald laser tunnels.

  • #BeerlaoGreenLIHF viral TikTok & Instagram Reels photo installation
  • Eco-Can Crusher challenge (crush 5 empty cans to earn free tokens)
  • Augmented Reality (AR) Instagram face filters with the Boombox head

Precision-timed programming delivering continuous entertainment, workshops, audience competitions, and headline concert performances.
